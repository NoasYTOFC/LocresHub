Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy describes how LocresHub, a desktop application for opening, searching, editing, and merging Unreal Engine .locres localization files, handles information when you use the application.

1. Files you work with

LocresHub is designed to process localization files and CSV files selected by you on your device. The application may save local preferences and work-in-progress data so you can continue where you left off. Your local files are not sent to the project maintainer by this website.

2. Google sign-in and Google Sheets

If you choose to use Google sign-in or an optional Google Sheets collaboration feature, Google may provide basic account information and the application may request permission to access Google resources needed for that feature. Access is subject to the permissions shown in Google's consent screen and the scopes configured for the application.

When you connect a spreadsheet, its contents are handled through Google services according to your authorization and Google's own terms and privacy policy. Other people with access to that spreadsheet may be able to view or edit its contents. Only connect spreadsheets you are comfortable sharing with their listed collaborators.

You can revoke the application's Google access from your Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future API access until you authorize it again.

3. Information collected by this website

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. The project does not provide a website account or a form for submitting localization files. GitHub may process technical data, such as request logs, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

4. Third-party services

Google services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. GitHub Pages is governed by GitHub's applicable policies. LocresHub does not control the privacy practices of these third-party services.

5. Data security and retention

Keep your computer and Google account secure, and avoid sharing OAuth tokens or credential files. Data stored in a Google spreadsheet remains subject to that spreadsheet's sharing settings and Google's retention practices. Local application data remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it through another service.

6. Children's privacy

LocresHub is a general-purpose localization utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children through this website.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as LocresHub's features change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision published here.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or the project, contact the maintainer through the LocresHub GitHub Issues page. Please do not post private account details, tokens, or sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Important: Before using this policy for Google's OAuth verification, confirm that every statement matches the app's actual behavior and the exact Google API scopes you request. Update this page whenever the app's data practices change.

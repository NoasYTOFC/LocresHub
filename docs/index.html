MADE FOR THE WORKFLOW

Everything in one focused editor.

No ads. No paywall. Just tools for working with localization files.

⌕

Find strings fast

Search groups and their text separately, with filters for edited and unedited entries.

✎

Edit with context

Keep the existing localized text visible while editing, and preserve source keys and hashes.

⇄

Collaborate through CSV

Export selected groups, share CSV files with teammates, and merge changes back into a LOCRES file.

▣

Work safely

Save an edited copy instead of overwriting the original file, and review conflicts during merges.

TEAMWORK, YOUR WAY

Share the work.
Keep control of your files.

LocresHub's CSV workflow lets teammates work on separate sets of strings and combine their changes. Google Sheets collaboration may be added as an optional workflow; when enabled, it will use the Google account and spreadsheet permissions you approve.
